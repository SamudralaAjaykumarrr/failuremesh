# Roadmap and validation gates

The [baseline's validation sequence](company/canonical-baseline-v1.0.md) is a sequence of hypotheses and evidence gates, not completed milestones. Phase 0 specifies PFC and AC concepts and verifier/safety/privacy invariants. Gate A — Corpus & Reference Architecture Bootstrap remains **REVISE**: 10/10 exact-family L0 mechanism mappings, 8/10 qualifying public observed incidents, and unresolved families 1 and 3. Its ten-incident validation milestone is incomplete, and generic incident searching is closed. The [first bounded-entry change](company/baseline-change-001-bounded-phase1-entry.md) authorizes only timeout-after-external-commit; the [second approved change](company/baseline-change-002-bounded-pfc2-entry.md) separately authorizes only duplicate queue delivery with a serial synthetic queue and PostgreSQL effects. Both are synthetic level-1 reference slices. No broader Phase 1, customer execution, or production execution is authorized. Gate A claims no reproduction or verification and implements no Phase 2+ work. Later V0 work may broaden to ten PFCs, three reference services, five fault primitives, and one CLI/proof format, but each expansion depends on evidence and separate authorization.

The canonical order after Gate A remains: develop the three specified reference architectures; make three PFCs executable; conduct five expert interviews; reproduce one real historical external incident, then three materially different historical incidents; find a design partner; run a pilot; reach first payment. None of these outcomes is claimed here. Product packaging and commercial scope should follow evidence, not precede it.

Kill or pivot if applicability stays noisy, false positives destroy trust, engineers refuse runner installation, failure mechanisms fail to transfer, experiments cannot be reproduced, or users see demo value without operational value. Record negative evidence and the decision before expanding the corpus or deployment surface.

Explicit current non-goals: SaaS dashboard, billing, enterprise RBAC/SSO, uncontrolled production chaos, automatic global incident ingestion, large connector ecosystem, AI-issued safety or proof verdicts, Phase 2+ implementation, and claims of broad portability or market validation. The baseline's possible future mesh-control and commercial features remain future possibilities.
