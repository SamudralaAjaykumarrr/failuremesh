# Architecture Packet Builder

Status: **architecture/specification only, for independent architecture review**. This stage specifies an Architecture Evidence Packet (AEP): a frozen, evidence-backed representation of one target/reference system from which later deterministic applicability evaluation can ask whether a known mechanism's prerequisites exist. No packet implementation, real corpus, retrieval, crawler, scraper, customer scan, matcher integration or execution capability is delivered or authorized.

The [canonical baseline](../company/canonical-baseline-v1.0.md), [invariants](../invariants.md), [system architecture](../architecture/system.md), [AC](../architecture/architecture-contract.md), [PFC](../architecture/pfc.md), [applicability semantics](../architecture/applicability.md) and [ADRs 0001–0006](../adr) remain authoritative. This specification refines the [foundation](phase-1-foundation.md), [evidence model](evidence-model.md), [evaluation protocol](evaluation-protocol.md), [counterfactual model](counterfactual-model.md) and [proof envelope](proof-bundle.md), without replacing them.

## Boundary and decisions

```text
independent public/synthetic architecture evidence + registered inventory policy
→ immutable source inventory and scoped candidate assertions
→ deterministic fact assessments + unresolved/contradictory facts
→ target-independent AC projection with complete derivation trace
→ reviewed input closure → frozen packet manifest + detached freeze receipt
→ append-only exposure/correction/retirement history
→ historical replay or current verification at a declared checkpoint
```

Collection independence is an evidence-bound claim, not a precondition assumed true. Informed or incomplete packets may be useful development material if honestly labeled. Unknowns are valid packet contents. Structural validity, evidence sufficiency, collection independence and replay availability are separate results.

The builder fits mesh-agent's future evidence-construction responsibility; mesh-registry retains immutable records. Versioned evidence-assessment and projection rules fit mesh-match's existing responsibility, but packet construction never invokes its applicability evaluator. There is no new service, execution adapter or governance role. LLMs may propose candidates; only pinned deterministic rules operating on admissible evidence determine final assessments and projection. Unsupported semantics remain UNKNOWN.

The design chooses a **versioned AEP sidecar around the conceptual AC**, with a separately hashed projection. It does not change AC schema 1, existing PFC bytes, legacy evaluator behavior or proof formats. The [current Phase 2A substrate](phase-2a-implementation.md) has only a synthetic retry predicate; it is not a general natural-architecture validator. The [Phase 2B register](phase-2b-implementation.md) supplies selection/exposure references, not fact authority. New schemas, predicates, parsers and any integration need separate implementation authorization.

The later deterministic-claim-validator stage expands predicate coverage; the builder consumes only explicitly supported, pinned rules and abstains elsewhere. It cannot make that later stage implicit by accepting caller-declared SUPPORTED states. A packet may freeze with semantic unknowns before broader validation exists, but cannot claim useful natural-architecture coverage from structural checks alone.

## Policy-first intake and independent inventory

Before intake, bind a policy ID/version/digest with system eligibility, inventory domains, allowed source classes, finite submitted-source/byte/analysis budgets, cutoff, actor roles, exposure rules, predicate/normalization/projection versions, freshness profiles, rights/classification rules and omission reasons. Selection is independent of target prerequisites wherever possible. No automatic selection based on likely matcher success. Every submitted source, alternative, rejection and failed submission through the cutoff is accounted for, including irrelevant, inaccessible, conflicting and weak material. Completeness is bounded to recorded submissions and declared search scope; the builder cannot prove undisclosed sources were not withheld.

A future first implementation accepts **explicitly supplied local public/synthetic evidence and references only**. Public repository/config/docs availability must have a recorded lawful-access and retention basis; public visibility is not permission. No network fetch, repository checkout, source-program execution, active diagram rendering, embedded commands, telemetry or automatic model dispatch. Existing permitted runtime observations may be referenced; the builder does not generate new observations. Natural-source, authored synthetic and synthetic-descendant origins stay distinct; synthetic content on a public URL remains synthetic.

Each intake entry binds:

| Group | Required information |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Logical source ID/revision, immutable source-record digest, raw artifact digest/length/representation if captured; uncaptured citation explicitly typed with no invented digest |
| Attribution | Canonical and actual locator, publisher and attribution basis, origin/project lineage, mirror/copy/archive/derived relationships and unresolved identity links |
| Acquisition | Supplier/collector/context/tool/build, reported capture time, original acquisition method and limitations; supplied bytes are not attested retrieval |
| Scope | System/project/component IDs, build/commit/release, configuration digest or explicit UNKNOWN, environment, operation/path, identity domain, quantifier, temporal interval, design-versus-observed basis |
| Source location | Exact artifact and selector representation; byte range or page/region/section/symbol with separately bound extraction and excerpt digests |
| Rights | Metadata/digest/excerpt/whole-body/export permissions separately, basis/license or UNKNOWN, retention deadline, classification and current check time |
| Disposition | Accepted for a named assessment, candidate-only, rejected or unavailable, with reasons and alternatives; acceptance into inventory never establishes a fact |
| Change history | Source supersession/correction/retraction, availability observations, old/new versions and dependent facts; no overwrite |

Use existing [source/claim semantics](evidence-model.md) and [corpus rights/dependence rules](corpus-record-model.md). Unknown sensitivity or suspected private/secret/PII material is refused/quarantined locally without model/export use. Publicly leaked private material is excluded. Retain only a safe opaque failed-attempt envelope when content/metadata retention is not permitted; do not hash prohibited payloads as a workaround. Rights govern prompts, diagnostics, caches and context manifests too. No source-text export is part of this stage. Expiry or required deletion takes precedence over retaining replay bytes: append a permitted tombstone and report reduced replay, never regenerate forbidden text.

### Source-specific limits

- **Diagrams:** bind original bytes, document revision/page/region, legend, node/edge labels, direction, extraction coordinates and tool. A visual/OCR/LLM reading is a candidate transcription. Unsupported symbol semantics stay UNKNOWN. A deterministic structured-diagram parser may establish declared nodes/edges under a pinned grammar, never infer durability, protocols or missing edges from shape, proximity or omission. A diagram is documented design unless separately bound evidence establishes deployment.
- **Repository/config:** bind repository/project lineage, exact commit, file bytes, path/selectors, build/config profile and imported defaults/version semantics. A key in a sample config establishes only that sample's setting. Establishing an effective setting requires all precedence layers/includes/defaults and their order; unknown environment overrides or dynamic code paths block certainty about effective deployment. Never execute build hooks or resolve dependencies over the network.
- **Docs/reports:** retain exact statements and reported/design scope. Citation presence and human approval do not establish semantic entailment. Unformalized prose stays an attributed candidate unless a reviewed predicate-specific evidence rule can check its exact meaning and scope. Vendor names, claimed guarantees and common architecture patterns never supply omitted facts.
- **Existing observations:** bind observer/query/tool identity, observed build/environment/interval, event identity, completeness and provenance limits. A public report of observation remains reported evidence; a local synthetic trace cannot prove upstream behavior. Observer access, causal fault placement and permission to execute are separate concepts.

Aliases and copied/mirrored sources share origin dependencies; multiple URLs or matching narratives do not create independent corroboration. Possible overlap prevents a claimed independent support count and conservatively joins leakage components. Cross-source identity mapping needs explicit evidence. Two components named `worker` in different projects cannot join by label. Unresolved joins remain separate candidate identities and block facts needing that join.

### Required broad inventory

For every declared system/scope, each domain has an inventory entry: scoped subjects/edges, source and fact refs, collection coverage, missing portions, limitations and reason. `REVIEWED_WITH_GAPS`, `NOT_EVIDENCED` and `BOUNDED_COVERAGE_ESTABLISHED` describe collection coverage, never applicability or truth. A domain cannot disappear because the eventual PFC does not mention it. “Not present” needs a scoped negative fact, not an empty list.

| Domain | Minimum questions retained even when UNKNOWN |
| --- | --- |
| Components/runtime | Stable namespaced identities, role, runtime/language/version, build/config/environment, operation entrypoints and ownership |
| Storage/transactions | Stores, durable writes, commit/ack boundaries, atomicity and transaction membership, external effects |
| Queues/streams | Producers/consumers, delivery/order/ack/redelivery semantics, identity/deduplication, retention and partitions |
| Network/trust boundaries | Directed calls, sync/async mode, protocol, caller/provider boundary, trust/network zones and uncertain links |
| Retries/timeouts | Actor, path, trigger, count/budget, backoff/jitter, deadlines/cancellation, logical identity across attempts |
| Consistency/durability | Read/write guarantees, acknowledgment meaning, persistence scope and stated failure assumptions |
| Replication | Replica roles, topology, quorum, lag, failover and recovery semantics with version/config scope |
| Concurrency/ownership | Overlap, synchronization, leases/expiry/reacquisition, fencing, ordering and authority |
| Caches | Keys, population/invalidation, lifetime, consistency, shared dependencies and miss coordination |
| Schedulers/workers | Dispatch, ownership, work lifecycle, restart/replay, scheduling and completion/ack boundaries |
| Failure/recovery | Failure detection, retry/restart/failover, reconciliation, recovery sequence and durable state boundaries |
| Dependency edges | Source/target IDs, operation, resource/capacity dependence, causal versus merely structural relation |
| Protective controls | Idempotency, dedupe, fencing, admission limits, isolation, circuit breaking and other controls; coverage, bypasses and evidence limits |
| Proof observability | Existing observers and coverage, identity correlation and synchronization evidence; unknown/unavailable capabilities separate from architecture facts |

A bounded coverage certificate lists the enumerated subject/path set, source closure, observer/parser rule, scope, exclusions and evidence that enumeration is complete. “All files scanned” alone does not show all runtime paths, overrides or external calls were covered. Inventory completeness and per-predicate negation completeness are different obligations. No globally “complete architecture” flag exists.

## Atomic facts and deterministic assessment

The atomic unit is a typed proposition: `(system, subject/edge, predicate, candidate value, exact scope, epistemic basis)`. Required fields include logical ID/revision/parent, source/location refs, candidate origin, extractor/model/prompt/context identity, rule versions, accepted/rejected supports, challenges, completeness arguments, freshness, derivation parents, effective state and reasons. Quantifiers are explicit: one bounded operation, a named finite path set, or a documented-design statement. Wider quantifiers require their own rule and coverage; the current Phase 2A quantifier is not silently expanded.

Normalization is deterministic and lossless with respect to audit: keep original names/units/selectors alongside normalized forms. Scope identity is a tuple, never ambiguous concatenation. Pinned rules define unit conversion, enum vocabulary and identity aliases; unrecognized values abstain. An ambiguous entity or inferred edge remains a candidate with UNKNOWN/UNSUPPORTED state, not an established inventory member. Derivations form an acyclic graph. No join crosses build, project, environment, operation, identity domain or epistemic basis without an explicit supported bridge rule.

Use the existing six states; these are evidence states, not six applicability verdicts:

| Effective state | Required basis | Projection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SUPPORTED_TRUE` | Current admissible positive evidence for exactly the proposition and scope | TRUE, or the supported typed value |
| `SUPPORTED_FALSE` | Current admissible evidence for its scoped negation, with required completeness | FALSE for that proposition only |
| `UNKNOWN` | Missing value, unresolved identity/interpretation/coverage or scope mapping | UNKNOWN with null value and reasons |
| `CONFLICTING` | Material unresolved contradictory evidence/challenge on a semantically shared scope, including partial overlap or containment | UNKNOWN on the affected scope; both sides retained |
| `STALE` | Otherwise sufficient evidence fails its bound validity profile | UNKNOWN; old support retained |
| `UNSUPPORTED` | AI-only assertion, unsupported predicate/type, invalid provenance or no admissible support | UNKNOWN; proposed value retained only as candidate |

For a typed assertion such as `delivery_mode = at_least_once`, SUPPORTED_FALSE refutes that equality; it does not invent another mode. Typed field projection requires a supported value. Mutually exclusive supported values conflict wherever their scopes semantically overlap under B1 below; many-valued predicates declare their cardinality explicitly.

Assessment runs over the **entire frozen inventory closure**, including rejected supports and challenges, never just the favorable refs supplied by a candidate. First validate structure/refs/target type and rule eligibility. Malformed packet closure refuses freeze/replay; a well-formed but ineligible semantic candidate is UNSUPPORTED. Then identify material conflicts, check freshness, scope and inference, and assign state as in the evidence model. Inspect every relevant support/challenge with deterministic reason ordering. Current material conflict blocks certainty; expired/out-of-scope disagreement remains visible with a rule-bound reason it does not block. A credible unresolved primary-evidence challenge blocks certainty even if automated entailment cannot settle it; an unsupported model disagreement alone is an advisory hypothesis. No vote, source prestige, model confidence or “reviewed” flag decides truth.

Split documented design and runtime scopes before resolving a diagram/config disagreement. This may preserve two different supported statements; it does not allow either to support a third deployed-scope claim. If both concern incompatible behavior on a semantically shared scope, retain CONFLICTING there until an evidence-bound correction/resolution rule applies. A supersedes link alone is not semantic correction. The newer publication does not automatically win. Preserve rejected interpretations and the reason for any scope split so a reviewer can challenge convenient reclassification.

Negation requires a named closed domain: for example, no retry on exactly path P under configuration C can be supported by a rule proving complete control-flow/config coverage of P, including library/proxy retries. A missing retry key, zero observed retries in an incomplete trace or a diagram with no queue cannot establish absence. A trace containing one attempt establishes no repeat only in its completely observed bounded operation, not “this service never retries.” Empty observations require an established observer, bounded observation interval and completeness; they cannot vacuously prove a system property.

Freshness uses either immutable build/config scope with no age-only TTL, or an explicit validity interval and invalidating events for moving facts. Bind the assessment time as an input. Omitted validity for moving state gives UNKNOWN; it never means forever. Source revision changes, retractions, build/config changes and material challenges append dependency invalidations. Historical replay retains the original policy/time; current checks do not reuse expired or withdrawn support.

### B1 — semantic scope relationships and contradictions

This section makes “same scope” a semantic comparison, not an exact-tuple filter. Distinguish an assertion's immutable record identity, its scope's canonical representation, and the region that scope denotes. Different scope IDs/bytes do not imply disjoint regions. Preliminary authority means support/admissibility established under the pinned rules before comparing assertions; it is not a final conflict-free assessment.

**Scope identity.** Bind the scope schema, system/project lineage, deployment/environment, build/configuration, epistemic basis, identity domain, subject selector, component/path/operation selectors, time selector and quantifier. Selectors use evidence-resolved namespaced IDs and explicit finite sets or a named bounded universe with a completeness certificate. Preserve correlations as a finite union of clauses, each denoting a product of its declared dimensions; never turn `(component C1, path A)` and `(C2, B)` into all four pairs. Sort/deduplicate sets by canonical ID. A hierarchy edge is not containment evidence by itself: expanding “all paths of component C” requires a versioned, complete membership mapping. Missing selectors are UNKNOWN, not wildcard, empty set, or “all.” A proved not-relevant dimension requires a predicate-specific rule. Empty scopes cannot support a fact or a negative by vacuity.

**Equality/overlap/containment.** The relationship record binds both scope IDs, normalization/membership rule IDs, evidence and a region relationship: EQUAL, A_CONTAINS_B, B_CONTAINS_A, PARTIAL_OVERLAP, DISJOINT, BASIS_SEPARATE or UNRESOLVED; when known it also binds intersection and both remainders. Full scope equality additionally requires the same basis and quantifier semantics, not merely matching display strings. Equal regions with different quantifiers still undergo the quantified contradiction rules below; unequal scope identities cannot suppress that comparison. For resolved finite sets, overlap is nonempty intersection and containment is subset inclusion. Apply this separately to subject/component/path/operation dimensions, preserving clause correlations. Different names, builds or config hashes alone do not prove separation: require evidenced distinct identities/versions or an explicit scope bridge. Proven distinct environments/components/paths can establish disjointness. Unknown aliases, membership or a required bridge give UNRESOLVED, unless another resolved dimension already proves disjointness.

Temporal behavior uses bounded half-open intervals `[start,end)` in a common established clock/timezone and precision, with `start < end`. For intervals A and B, overlap is `max(A.start,B.start) < min(A.end,B.end)`; containment A contains B is `A.start <= B.start` and `B.end <= A.end`; equality requires equal endpoints. Touching endpoints are disjoint. For interval unions apply those operations to normalized constituent intervals. Unknown endpoints, timezone, clock correspondence or insufficient precision give UNRESOLVED where overlap cannot be proved or excluded. A pinned immutable-build scope uses a distinct temporal tag, not an invented infinite interval; relating it to a deployment interval requires evidence connecting that build/configuration to that deployment/time. Source publication/capture time and evidence freshness are not behavioral scope endpoints.

For product clauses, one proved disjoint dimension is enough for DISJOINT; otherwise every relevant dimension must be resolved to claim equality, containment or a nonempty intersection. Compute union/intersection/difference over the clauses, not label similarity. A narrower subject selector cannot escape comparison with a containing selector. With unresolved relationships, mark every potentially affected region UNKNOWN with `SCOPE_RELATION_UNRESOLVED`; use resolved dimensions to bound that region. If no narrower bound can be proved, the whole affected requested scope is UNKNOWN. Do not assume disjointness or select a favored assertion.

**Quantifiers and contradiction eligibility.** A predicate rule declares value equivalence/incompatibility, quantifier, whether it distributes over scope, and any allowed bridge. The bounded default `FOR_EACH_THROUGHOUT` asserts the value for every listed member throughout the interval; a negative additionally requires its complete observation/path/config basis. It restricts to a subregion only with retained support and restrictable completeness. `EXISTS` establishes a witness, not a value throughout the scope; bind the witnessed member/time/operation. A universal negative conflicts with an opposing witness in its region. If an existential is supported only within a bounded region wholly contained in the negative, their conjunction is inconsistent but its exact location is unresolved: the whole possible witness region fails closed (UNKNOWN with a contradiction-location reason), never invent a point or distribute the witness. If its possible region only partly overlaps and no witness can be localized, mark the possible overlap UNKNOWN rather than invent a contradiction or assume a witness outside it. Bounded operation observations keep their run/operation identity and cannot become universal path behavior. Aggregates, cardinalities or other non-distributive predicates require an explicit quantified incompatibility/restriction rule; without one the affected relation is UNKNOWN. In particular, aggregate zero is a broad absence claim only when a complete nonnegative counting rule establishes that equivalence.

Assertions are eligible to contradict if they have current admissible support in the compared region, concern the same registered proposition or a declared semantic equivalence/incompatibility, have compatible identity/basis under evidenced bridges, and cannot both hold under their quantifiers on shared scope. Exact interval/path-set equality is unnecessary. Predicate names alone cannot establish compatibility or incompatibility. B2 handles different predicates that meet only at a common destination. Unsupported AI assertions remain non-authoritative hypotheses; credible unresolved evidence challenges retain the existing fail-closed treatment.

Documented design intent and observed deployment behavior intentionally denote different epistemic worlds: record BASIS_SEPARATE and preserve both assertions without promoting either to the other. Basis is determined from evidence and rule, not chosen to evade conflict. A claimed bridge or ambiguity about what a source asserts makes the affected deployed view UNKNOWN until resolved. Extraction method/source kind is not an automatic separation: two admissible reports/observations about the same deployed fact must be compared once their evidence-bound bases refer to the same world. Proven disjoint environments, intervals, components or paths are legitimate non-conflicts; unproved differences are not.

**Partial conflict and completeness.** For distributive facts, partition comparable regions using all resolved interval endpoints and subject/path membership boundaries from the complete input set. Each canonical cell is a namespaced member tuple plus elementary interval (or established pinned-build region); it records all covering assertions. On every cell with incompatible authoritative values set CONFLICTING and project UNKNOWN. This mask applies to both assertions, including a broader negative: neither side wins the overlap. Unaffected remainders retain support only if their original evidence and completeness independently restrict there. Whole-scope summaries cannot hide a conflicting/unknown cell; a universal negative spanning one is no longer supported over its original scope. Cells are sorted only after resolution; traversal order cannot choose values or partitions. Known-different values on disjoint cells are scoped variation, not a contradiction; a requested single uniform value across them is UNKNOWN with `NONUNIFORM_SCOPE`.

Contrary narrower evidence invalidates a coverage certificate's use to certify the original negative. Record affected certificate ID, contradicted premise and scope. If the evidence reveals an omitted path, invalid observer or broken shared completeness premise, all claims depending on that premise become UNKNOWN, including apparently unaffected remainders. If certificate evidence proves separable coverage for the remainder and an explicit restriction rule permits subtraction, derive a new narrowed certificate referencing the original, the removed cells and contrary evidence; do not edit or continue using the old certificate as globally valid. Without that proof, no supported negative survives by automatic subtraction. Pure membership enumeration need not itself be false when behavior contradicts it, but enumeration alone cannot certify absence. Keep independently admitted contradiction evidence in the assessment even if a completeness dependency is subsequently invalidated; do not erase the conflict by dropping the negative from history. Propagate masks and invalidated premises through every derived fact/view that uses them before AC projection. Historical packet bytes and their original assessments remain unchanged; new evidence changes only a successor/current assessment.

## Architecture Contract projection

The frozen projection covers declared operation views from the broad inventory, selected without target-PFC filtering. Its input is the exact fact-assessment set, scope selector, predicate registry and mapping policy. Its output is an evidence-bearing conceptual AC view plus trace, explicit unknown fields and unmapped candidates. No model-produced AC is accepted as the authoritative output.

Every mapping rule declares source predicate/type, accepted epistemic basis and source classes, scope/quantifier join conditions, permitted transformations, target AC domain/field, completeness needs, conflict handling and stable refusal codes. Pin rule bytes and digest, not just a display name. There is no default rule for unknown predicates and no generic natural-language truth rule. A future implementation proposal must enumerate its supported rule set and golden examples; until then no natural fact support is claimed by this specification.

| AC destination | Deterministic projection obligation |
| --- | --- |
| Identity/build/environment/runtime | Copy supported scoped identity/runtime facts; unresolved identities block joins. Never substitute reference fixture metadata |
| Databases/transaction/provider boundaries | Project typed store/effect/transaction memberships and edges; derive separation only from evidenced distinct commit boundaries |
| Queue/worker/lease/concurrency fields | Copy supported per-edge/worker semantics under exact scope; component existence alone cannot establish delivery, expiry or fencing semantics |
| Retry/backoff/jitter/timeout/identity | Project actor/path/trigger/count/identity relation individually; unresolved default/override makes affected effective values UNKNOWN |
| Consistency/durability/replication/cache/dependencies | Preserve guarantee scope, failure assumptions, topology and per-edge uncertainty; vendor label is never a rule |
| Idempotency/reconciliation/other controls | Copy evidence and coverage as controls; do not derive mechanism absence, exclusion or resilience |
| Observability/causal capabilities | Copy only supported capability statements with provenance; no architecture diagram supplies a fault hook or complete runtime observer |
| Boolean predicates in a later compatibility view | TRUE only from SUPPORTED_TRUE, FALSE only from SUPPORTED_FALSE for the same proposition; all other states become null/UNKNOWN |

Projection must follow the B1+B2 order below. No mapping writes directly to a destination before contributor collection and resolution. Unrelated unknowns do not erase a separately supported field, but a participating unresolved contributor to that field does. A derived negation cannot be obtained by taking the complement of UNKNOWN. Composite predicates must pin three-valued truth tables and coverage requirements, not treat null as false; these are distinct from competing mappings of the same destination.

### B2 — mandatory cross-rule destination resolution

**Destination identity and registry.** Canonical destination identity is `(AC schema/profile, system/deployment identity, epistemic basis, scoped view/cell, canonical field-path segments, addressed subject/path/map-key IDs)`. It excludes rule ID and source predicate, so different rules cannot create separate destinations for the same field. Field aliases resolve through the pinned schema. Unresolved destination identity is a semantic UNKNOWN for every bounded potentially affected slot; malformed paths, ambiguous schema aliases or unknown types are registry/structural failures. Partition overlapping destination scopes under B1 before comparing values: unequal whole-view IDs must not hide a common cell.

Every destination schema declares scalar/set/list/map kind, element/value types, units, equality, cardinality, and whether it represents a complete value or partial member/key observations. Scalars have one value per cell. Sets compare canonical membership, independent of order; lists compare complete ordered sequences, retaining duplicate multiplicity; maps compare canonical keys and their values, with key-universe completeness separate from per-key facts. Length/cardinality are distinct scalar destinations derived only from complete collections. No empty collection proves absence without complete scope coverage, and no implicit set union, list concatenation or map overwrite is allowed.

Each writable field template has exactly one declared resolution group listing all allowed mapping rules/source predicates, scope selectors, conversions, required versus optional contributor slots, and aggregation profile. Registry validation must analyze potential template/alias/selector overlap before input processing. Multiple potentially colliding rules must belong to the same declared group or have a mechanically checkable disjointness proof; undecidable registry overlap without a shared group fails validation. An undeclared actual collision always refuses the projection even if values agree. A later rule addition changes the registry version. Stable rule order is not a conflict policy.

V1 uses **agreement of complete values** for scalar and whole set/list/map destinations. Intentional multiple sources must explicitly declare that profile. A member/key projection is a separately addressed destination using the same agreement rule; it cannot imply the whole collection is complete. Any future aggregation with different semantics requires its own reviewed profile with typed inputs, complete contributor enumeration, missing-input behavior and permutation-invariant output. No unspecified aggregation runs under v1. Source dependence remains in the trace; agreeing copies do not become independent corroboration or additional votes.

**Collect before resolve.** Enumerate every declared rule against the complete frozen fact/candidate inventory and B1 assessment masks, not just facts listed by a proposed winning mapping. Produce a contributor record for every matching rule/input tuple with source fact IDs/revisions, mapping-rule ID/version/digest, destination, input/effective scope, restriction/bridge references, transform, semantic value or null, state, evidence/dependence and reasons. Include source UNKNOWN/CONFLICTING/STALE states and rule-relevant candidates with unresolved admissibility/semantics as unresolved contributions. A declared required slot with no input produces a null placeholder with `MISSING_REQUIRED_INPUT`. Optional means no placeholder when no candidate exists; it never permits ignoring an unfavorable existing contributor.

Separate a **participating unresolved contribution** from a **proved ineligible candidate**. A stale once-admissible source relevant to the destination participates as UNKNOWN; expiry alone is not proof of irrelevance. A candidate whose source rule/semantics are unsupported or unresolved participates as UNKNOWN if it could support this declared destination and has a credible evidence basis. Proven wrong-scope/basis inputs and AI-only assertions with no admissible underlying evidence are excluded with explicit reasons, never an authoritative contradiction or vote. A registered rule with unresolved eligibility cannot label its input irrelevant. An unregistered predicate has no writer; retain it as unmapped and, if a credible unresolved challenge could affect a destination, propagate B1's UNKNOWN mask there. No contributor is promoted by a caller-supplied status. Invalid bytes/references remain structural failures, not exclusions.

**Resolve each cell as one set operation.** Normalize values using declared exact conversions and semantic equality; retry count counts retries after the initial attempt, so a supported `total_attempts = n` maps to `n - 1` only when its rule establishes the same operation/budget meaning, one initial attempt and integer `n >= 1`. A configured maximum cannot silently equal an observed attempt count. Missing conversion premises produce UNKNOWN, not subtraction by guess. Known invalid rule definitions/types refuse registry validation.

| Complete contributor group on a resolved cell | Destination result under v1 |
| --- | --- |
| One authoritative value, no unresolved contributor/required gap | That typed value, with its sole support trace |
| Multiple authoritative semantically equal values, no unresolved contributor | That value with every contributor, not a selected winner |
| Two incompatible authoritative values, with or without UNKNOWN contributors | UNKNOWN with CONFLICTING resolution and all alternatives |
| TRUE plus a participating UNKNOWN | UNKNOWN; no boolean OR or confidence shortcut |
| FALSE plus a participating UNKNOWN | UNKNOWN; no boolean AND or mechanism-absence inference |
| Any other known value plus a participating UNKNOWN, stale relevant input or unresolved supported-rule input | UNKNOWN; retain the known candidate and every blocking reason |
| Conflicting source fact on that cell | UNKNOWN with inherited conflict, even if another mapping supplies a known value |
| Only unresolved contributors, or no authoritative value after recorded exclusions | UNKNOWN; missing evidence does not become FALSE or an empty collection |
| One known value plus only proved ineligible candidates | Known value if all required slots are satisfied; exclusions remain auditable |
| Partially overlapping contributor scopes | Apply B1 cells first; resolve all covering/potentially covering contributors on each cell. Uncovered destination portions are UNKNOWN; whole-view uniform value requires complete coverage and the same known value on every cell |

Conflict dominates the resolution reason, then unresolved contributions, then unanimous supported value; retain all other reasons regardless of that ordering. Incompatible values MUST NOT be resolved by prestige, confidence, model preference, rule order, first/last writer or majority vote. A field's UNKNOWN is a valid partial projection; registry failure, invalid closure/type/identity binding, undeclared collision, exceeded partition/resource bounds, or a frozen projection/trace inconsistent with recomputation refuses projection/freeze/verification entirely. No partial successful projection artifact is published on refusal. Semantic disagreements alone do not require discarding a valid packet with explicit unknowns.

### Mandatory B1+B2 processing and binding order

After schema/closure/registry validation, the only permitted order is:

1. Establish preliminary evidence-backed assertions and unresolved candidates, with source freshness/admissibility and quantifiers under the frozen policy/time. These are not yet conflict-free final facts.
2. Determine semantic scope relationships across all relevant assertions and declared destination scopes, including containment and possible overlap; bind relations, intersections/remainders and unresolved reasons. Registry-declared scope transforms/bridges participate here before any destination value is resolved.
3. Identify contradictions/unresolved overlap, partition canonical cells, invalidate or restrict completeness dependencies, and propagate affected states through derivations in dependency order. Retain every support and challenge; no early favorable selection.
4. Determine projection eligibility per cell: final supported facts may supply values; unresolved facts/masks may supply only unresolved contributions; prove every exclusion.
5. Collect ALL contributors and required-input placeholders for each canonical AC destination, including cross-rule and partially overlapping contributors.
6. Apply its declared aggregation/conflict semantics to the entire group. Resolve all cells before making any whole-view coverage/uniformity claim.
7. Emit deterministic typed values or explicit UNKNOWN with states/reasons, or refuse for the structural/registry failures above. This produces no applicability, exposure, resilience, mechanism-absence, safety or failure-prevention verdict; an evidenced scoped negative remains only that fact.
8. Bind source fact revisions, scope relations/cells, completeness changes, every contributing/excluded rule/input, registry/group/profile, values/unknowns and resolution trace into the frozen packet and separately hashed AC projection.

The verifier independently repeats this order from the bound inventory and registry. It must not trust a manifest's winning value, contributor list, eligibility flag or saved resolution. Missing a contender, laundering partial overlap into a different view ID, or altering a conflict trace fails verification even if an output hash has been updated. Canonical sorting occurs for serialization, not selection; partition and group results are functions of the complete input set. Every permutation of source/rule traversal produces identical canonical cells, contributor groups, decisions and output bytes. Bounds refuse excessive partitioning rather than drop conflicts. Historical verification uses the original versions/time only; current/revised projection recollects and reassesses all contributors under its explicit versions/time and never edits the old result.

These illustrative field paths define mapping obligations for the future conceptual projection, not existing Go fields or new source observations:

| Scoped input under a supported rule | Projection and required counterexample |
| --- | --- |

A partial packet remains partial in every view. No adapter or matcher call is added here. Packet and projection may be inspectable even when no existing evaluator accepts them.

## Exposure and dependence

Use the [corpus exposure protocol](corpus-selection-protocol.md), extending its records by reference rather than rewriting corpus history. Record both actual and available access for every selector, source supplier/collector, extractor, normalizer, identity resolver, policy author, reviewer, projector and freeze actor/tool/model context. Enumerate every actor/context/category cell before aggregation; a missing cell becomes UNKNOWN. This prevents an unrecorded collector from disappearing behind a known unexposed reviewer.

Required dimensions are **target PFC, matcher rules, expected applicability result, prior case outcome, causal-family label, previous benchmark/evaluation result**, plus architecture-packet access as in the corpus protocol. Bind artifact IDs/versions when known, occurrence time or UNKNOWN, recorded time, access restriction/basis, context inheritance, aliases, project/derivative parents and selection round. Prior case outcome means evaluation/reproduction feedback; incident outcome cues remain separately disclosed. Family-informed collection must say so even if no PFC was selected.

Effective state is the conservative union across actual and available access, all relevant actors/contexts, revisions, parents and leakage components: **YES > UNKNOWN > NO_WITH_BASIS**. NO_WITH_BASIS requires a bounded documented withholding/access arrangement; a denial of memory or a different model/session alone is insufficient. Historical YES cannot be cleared by renaming a system, changing tools, splitting a cluster or revising a packet. Model pretraining knowledge is always a separate UNKNOWN disclosure. The design authors have read PFCs and rules; illustrative cases here are informed synthetic design examples, not independently collected packets.

Report each dimension, and separately whether collection was target-informed, rule-informed, family-informed or prospective under recorded restrictions. A bounded target/rule-uninformed prospective claim requires NO_WITH_BASIS for every relevant actor/context in the five target/rule/expected-result/prior-outcome/benchmark dimensions. Family exposure is separately disclosed; a family-uninformed claim additionally requires NO_WITH_BASIS there. Reading the packet being constructed is recorded architecture access, not automatically target leakage. None of these statuses proves no undisclosed knowledge. Existing corpus fresh-split rules still apply, including their explicit family-informed selection allowance. Packet family exposure cannot be hidden behind corpus eligibility. All mirrors/revisions/descendants stay in their inherited allocation component; possible links prevent fresh independence claims. Later revealed access appends a notice that can invalidate current independence reporting without editing the frozen snapshot.

## Lifecycle and publication

`DRAFT → REVIEWED → FROZEN → EXPOSED → RETIRED`, with `FROZEN → RETIRED` also allowed. Supersession links an old packet to a new draft/review/freeze; retirement and exposure remain monotonic historical events. EXPOSED here means **evaluation-information exposure**, never the execution verdict EXPOSED. Already informed packets retain that status at freeze; lifecycle labels cannot erase pre-freeze access. Reviewed means the recorded construction review is complete, not independently approved, factually complete or safe.

1. **Draft:** append intake, candidates, assessments and revisions. Each mutation checks expected head; refused submissions retain safe accounting. Changes never replace published revisions.
2. **Reviewed:** publish a review artifact bound to exact inventory/fact/policy/projection/exposure digests. Record reviewer role/context, checks, unresolved challenges and limitations. Mechanical verification can complete a construction review; human involvement is not truth authority or new execution approval. Any changed input makes the review stale and requires a new review.
3. **Frozen:** require a closed inventory through a cutoff/head, every domain entry, exact references or typed citation limitations, full actor matrix, valid rights at supplied check time, acyclic lineage, recomputed states/projection/coverage summaries, current review and explicit limitations. Semantic unknowns/partial coverage are allowed; hidden gaps, false completeness, inconsistent summaries, invalid bindings or prohibited retention refuse freeze. A frozen packet is evidence for later evaluation, not evidence sufficient for every prerequisite.
4. **Exposed:** append access/reveal events before any later evaluation can claim withholding. The builder grants no evaluation authority and cannot prevent undisclosed external runs. Record locally observed order and limits honestly.
5. **Retired/superseded:** append reason, affected dependencies and successor where present; original bytes, review and receipt stay addressable subject to retention. Correction of evidence, scope, facts, policy or projection creates a new revision with full parents and exposure. No corrected packet can replace an exposed holdout as a fresh case.

Declare reviewer and intended freeze actor contexts in the reviewed input closure before publishing the review. Review and freeze receipts are administrative successors, not members of their own reviewed inputs. A different freeze actor or new exposure/source after review invalidates that review; append updated inputs and review again. New sources after the inventory cutoff enter a successor inventory/version, not the old frozen closure.

Publication follows the corpus commit-point pattern: under a cooperating single-writer lock, check expected head, durably publish immutable content and logical revision bindings, validate closure, publish manifest, then append a detached freeze receipt. Receipt publication is the commit point. Manifest binds the preceding ledger head and supplied freeze time, never its own receipt. Receipt binds manifest digest and previous head. Exact operation retries are idempotent; conflicting retries/stale writers fail. Crash/orphan/pending-refusal states are explicit and block new freeze until reconciled; recovery cannot silently register objects. Failed refusal persistence is a durability error, not acknowledged intake. Bounded-reader and malformed-input failures need the same safe submission accounting without retaining rejected bytes.

Hashes detect edits relative to known roots; they do not prevent administrator replacement, deletion or forged local chronology. Initial custody is **SELF_REPORTED**, matching Phase 2B, with no independent attestation or evaluation write-before-run enforcement. Trusted local storage/cooperating writers are an explicit limitation. Receipt anchoring, stronger custody or evaluation registration require separately authorized work.

## Manifest and deterministic bytes

Proposed `aep-manifest-v1` references typed immutable payloads. Each payload has schema/kind, logical ID, positive revision, parent digest or first-revision null, producer/tool/build, policy digest, supplied UTC time and classification. Sets have stable unique IDs; revisions are consecutive with one committed head, no cycles or conflicting identity bindings. IDs are opaque and never unchecked file paths. Every unknown uses explicit state, null value and reason; empty strings do not supply evidence.

| Manifest binding | Required payload/meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Policy and scope | Policy/version/digest, architecture/system/project identity, build/config/environment/basis, operation views, declared intended use and cutoff |
| Evidence inventory | Every submitted source/revision/disposition and safe refusal through cutoff; source-record refs, exact artifact digests/lengths/representations where captured; citation-only limits |
| Normalized inventory | All domain entries, component/edge identities and unresolved aliases, collection coverage and bounded completeness certificates |
| Facts and uncertainty | Candidate and assessment digests, accepted/rejected evidence, B1 scope relations/canonical cells and conflict masks, invalidated/narrowed completeness certificates, unknown/stale/unsupported reasons, derivation DAG and fixed assessment time |
| AC projection | Target schema/profile, projection bytes/digest, destination identities/cardinalities, B2 registry resolution groups/profiles, all contributor and exclusion records with fact/rule IDs, per-cell/whole-view decisions, mapping/validator/predicate rule bytes and versions, unmapped fields and compatibility limits |
| Exposure/dependence | Actor/context/category matrix, effective states, pretraining UNKNOWN, origin/project/derivative links, corpus references and inherited split history where present, pre-freeze ledger head |
| Rights and availability | Per-source permission/basis/check/deadline, retained/external/transient dependencies, availability checkpoint, retention/export and audit limitations |
| Revision/review | Packet logical ID/revision, parent packet roots, correction reasons, synthetic parents, reviewed-input digest and review artifact |
| Encoding and tool | Schema/encoding/hash versions, exact tool/rule/build/toolchain identities and dirty-tree digest when applicable, input ordering and no-randomness declaration |
| Creation/freeze | Supplied creation/freeze times, actor/context, self-reported chronology tier; detached receipt required for registered-frozen status |
| Limits and replay | Domain deficits, known source omissions, unsupported rules, legacy compatibility, audit/replay tier and all required external evidence dependencies |

The root is `sha256:<digest of exact manifest bytes>`; root identity is detached, not a self-hash field. Payloads use local IDs/references, not the eventual root. Manifest records payload role/path/media type/size/digest and required/optional status. Factual inventory and AC projection have distinct hashes. Parent roots refer only to earlier published packets; post-freeze notices refer back without changing the root.

Required metadata/assessment/projection objects must exist. Source bodies may be explicitly external or unavailable with conditional replay limits, but evidence required by a semantic rule cannot be relabeled optional to preserve support. A freeze claiming supported facts must validate their evidence then, including permitted transient parents; subsequent loss preserves the historical assertion while blocking fresh semantic verification. Revisions of one logical packet have one immediate revision parent; other derivation/source-packet parents are separately typed, preserving multi-parent exposure without ambiguous revision heads.

Use a new pinned `aep-go-json-v1` profile consistent with [proof byte identity](proof-bundle.md): UTF-8, explicit fields/nulls, finite integer numeric fields, Go JSON escaping, no trailing newline, struct declaration order fixed by schema, set arrays sorted by ID then revision/digest using bytewise order, ordered evidence/history arrays preserved. Durations use integer value plus explicit unit; timestamps use canonical UTC with declared precision and UNKNOWN for unestablished source times. No locale, ambient clock, filesystem order, network lookup, model sampling or random seed enters replay. Unknown/duplicate keys, unsupported versions, duplicate IDs, invalid UTF-8, alternate encodings and noncanonical re-encodings are refused. Exact field layouts and golden bytes are a future implementation-entry deliverable, not implemented schemas here.

For initial implementation design, cap each encoded object/manifest at 4 MiB, source refs per packet at 256, fact candidates at 4096, domain/view entries at 4096, exposure cells at 8192, dependency closure at 16384 objects/64 MiB and graph depth at 128. Supplied transient source parents count toward the 64 MiB processing budget and each is at most 4 MiB. Limit overflow refuses the operation with safe accounting; never truncate evidence, omit conflicts or split packets to claim complete coverage. Larger allowed evidence needs a separately reviewed policy/profile. Use regular files under a trusted local root; reject traversal, escaping/symlink paths, active content execution and unbounded decompression. Binary diagrams are inert artifacts, not executable render instructions.

## Verification and replay contract

Verification reports separately `integrity`, `inventory_coverage`, `fact_assessment_status`, `projection_consistency`, `information_exposure`, `current_use_status`, `audit_capability`, `chronology_tier` and reason/limitation lists. There is no overall PASS meaning truth. Malformed closure is an integrity failure, not a successfully evaluated UNKNOWN fact. A valid packet containing unsupported facts is successful structural processing with explicit semantic unknowns. Unavailable evidence blocks the affected assessment/replay; do not pretend a saved success flag is fresh verification.

**Historical mode** takes exact root, bound policy/rule/tool bytes, frozen assessment time and ledger prefix containing its receipt. Verify hashes/sizes/schema, complete intake closure/revision ancestry, scope joins/DAGs, rights as recorded, exposure matrix, inventory summaries, assessments and projection. Use only original source revisions. An observed later correction appears as a separate notice when supplied, never changes the historical result. Historical decoding confers no permission to read expired/restricted content today.

**Current mode** additionally requires an explicit current policy-check time and verified latest available local notice/exposure/dependence head, plus source availability/rights checkpoints. Reject a caller-supplied stale local head. Apply all relevant corrections, withdrawals, retirement, rights loss, contamination and build/scope invalidations through that head. Emit a new assessment/report referencing original root and current head; a changed projection needs a successor packet before later use. “Current” is only as of these declared inputs; offline checks cannot prove no newer external facts or notices exist. Without latest-head access, report historical-only/current UNKNOWN, not current eligibility. A hash alone never renews staleness or semantic support.

Exactly reproducible with identical permitted input bytes, rules, times and heads:

1. Source/record identities and byte selectors, normalized component/edge IDs and typed values from supported deterministic extraction rules.
2. All fact states, accepted/rejected support reasons, conflicts, freshness, completeness and dependency traces.
3. AC projection bytes, trace, unmapped/unknown fields and projection digest.
4. Inventory closure/counts/deficits, dependence components, exposure union, revision/lifecycle validity and manifest bytes/root when supplied metadata is identical.
5. Historical/current verification report bytes under their respective pinned inputs, with distinct capability/error statuses.

An LLM's candidate-generation text is an immutable **input**, not a step promised to regenerate. Assessment must be independently reproducible from cited evidence and rules without calling that model. Unsupported OCR/prose interpretation remains unresolved; replaying a saved interpretation only establishes that the same interpretation was recorded. Full source-to-fact replay requires permitted original/transient parent bytes and every extraction dependency. Phase 2A excerpt rules still require real parent bytes to verify ranges/length/coverage; citations and excerpt hashes cannot bypass that. No fake target claim may be created to fit its Record API.

Declare tiers separately: `METADATA_INTEGRITY_ONLY`, `CONDITIONAL_EVIDENCE_REPLAY` (named missing/permitted external dependencies), `FULL_EVIDENCE_DECISION_REPLAY` (all required bytes/rules available). Even full replay establishes deterministic consistency of premises, not source truth, source independence, runtime behavior or trusted chronology. Network reacquisition is outside this builder; later differently hashed source bytes create a revision, never repair a historical replay silently. Experiment reproduction is outside every tier.

## Stop conditions and limits of the approach

Stop freeze/current use immediately for hidden submissions, invalid provenance/closure, prohibited retention, wrong-system joins, projection inconsistency, irreproducible decisions, silent exposure reset or an unsupported determinate field. Preserve safe failure history and affected roots; fixing one case does not erase its earlier use. A missing fact alone is not a design failure: it is an explicit UNKNOWN and may limit usefulness.

Require architecture redesign before expanding this stage if counterexamples show any of the following:

- A necessary fact can only be made determinate by model intuition, reviewer preference, hidden incident knowledge or a source-to-runtime scope leap the evidence rules cannot express.
- Broad inventories repeatedly omit material components/controls or cannot represent contradictory/time-dependent behavior without flattening it into one convenient boolean.
- Scoped negatives cannot be audited for complete boundaries, or protected architectures must be mislabeled as mechanism absence to fit the projection.
- Independent packet collection cannot meet the recorded withholding policy, origin clustering collapses claimed diversity, or known informed ancestors can be relabeled fresh.
- Useful decisive evidence cannot be retained/recovered under rights rules, or deterministic assessment depends on mutable network/model output; full replay claims must stop, not be weakened silently.
- Two clean replays of identical complete inputs disagree, historical replay silently adopts newer facts, or the required compatibility adapter loses scope/unknowns to fit a legacy evaluator.

For later empirical feasibility use the foundation's pre-registered budgets and thresholds: over 10% overturned decisive fact assessments, recurrent scope/identity errors producing certainty, or more than half of eligible natural pairings UNKNOWN after one budgeted repair round trigger its investigation requirements. This document records no such measurement and authorizes no collection round. Report counts, cases, competing explanations and a bounded redesign proposal. Any thesis change remains **PROPOSED BASELINE CHANGE** with the baseline's required rationale and evidence; no automatic governance or family substitution follows.

## Claim boundaries and governance

| Term | What this packet can record | What it cannot conclude |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Architecture evidence | Attributed source bytes/locations and existing observations, with provenance/rights | That a source is true, independent or currently deployed merely because available |
| Architecture facts | Rule-supported scoped assertions and explicit uncertainty | Universal topology/behavior or model intuition as evidence |
| AC projection | Deterministic evidence-bound representation under named rules | That any PFC applies or that an experiment is authorized |
| Applicability | No verdict; later mesh-match consumes exact PFC/AC and evidence scope | APPLICABLE/NOT_APPLICABLE from packet freeze, controls or missing evidence |
| Information exposure | Known/unknown access to rules, labels, targets and outcomes | The execution verdict EXPOSED or guaranteed blindness |
| Failure exposure | At most references to separately scoped prior evidence with origin limits | That this system is EXPOSED from its architecture alone |
| Resilience | Protective controls and their evidence/coverage | PROVEN_RESILIENT without later authorized finite execution and proof |
| Safety | Current authority limits and absence of execution capability | That the system is safe or permission to execute |

An Architecture Packet MUST NOT itself claim **the PFC applies, the system is exposed, the system is resilient, or the system is safe**. Those questions require the later deterministic evaluation/proof and safety boundaries; universal safety is never a permitted claim. Missing evidence never becomes FALSE.
