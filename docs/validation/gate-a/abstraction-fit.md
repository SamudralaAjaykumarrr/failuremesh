# Abstraction-fit review and canonical incident audit

These are L0 conceptual mappings, not machine-readable verified PFCs. **Exact** means the public record supports the family's defining causal mechanism at the stated evidence level; it does not require an evidenced downstream business impact and does not mean FailureMesh reproduction or portability. Project bug reports and technical analyses can establish mechanism fit, but only real publicly documented incidents or observed operational failures satisfy the separate canonical incident milestone. Unsupported downstream effects remain UNKNOWN. P/C checks whether prerequisites can be separated from controls-under-test. V/I/E/P/S checks whether a vendor-independent sequence, measurable invariant, platform-neutral experiment, proof requirements, and safety restrictions can be stated. Conditional means decisive incident evidence or architecture facts are missing.

All ten canonical families have exact defining-mechanism mappings at L0. Eight have qualifying incident sources; families 1 and 3 remain unresolved after the final search pass. Family 1 uses a WordPress project bug report whose injected response loss follows a completed media creation and precedes an automatic retry and second attachment; this is not payment-incident or independent FailureMesh evidence. Family 7 uses Dodo Payments’ first-party retrospective reporting lost webhooks after successful, committed payments; it is aggregate operational evidence without a per-payment forensic trace. Its numerical rate is approximate and its article contains an inconsistent chart label. Prior family-1 and family-7 sources remain near-matches. [Stripe](https://stripe.com/blog/idempotency) and [AWS outbox](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/cloud-design-patterns/transactional-outbox.html) documents remain supplementary mechanism guidance. The remaining mappings retain their prior scope and limitations, including UNKNOWN downstream effects where not source-established.

Where evidenced, prerequisites stay distinct from controls-under-test: placeable/observable effect and retry path versus idempotency; queue and redelivery versus dedupe; lease and sink versus fencing; finite workload/dependency versus backoff, pool budgets, circuit breaking or coalescing. Control presence alone cannot establish `NOT_APPLICABLE`. No hypothetical customer AC was evaluated.

The existing conceptual PFC field groups accommodate all ten supported sequences without a thesis change or a demonstrated architecture-level blocker to attempting the scoped first slice. Open questions for machine-readable encoding remain: feedback/capacity domains, multi-edge causal graphs and deadlines, ownership epochs at sinks, transport-specific acknowledgements, and scoped convergence windows. These are weaknesses, not changes to [PFC v1](../../architecture/pfc.md). Weak incident evidence is not grounds to redesign the model.

The final incident pass adds Apache Iceberg production observations for family 10 without changing its abstract commit-before-ack mechanism or the PFC/AC fit conclusion. Kafka control-topic offset advancement is the acknowledgement frontier in that architecture. The report does not justify treating the same documented incident as a second canonical incident for family 3. Family 1 and family 3 still lack qualifying incident evidence; this is an evidence gate, not a demonstrated architecture-level blocker.
