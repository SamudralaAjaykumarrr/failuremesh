# Expert review scorecard template

Use one copy per reviewer. Record every dimension, including negative, missing, and contradictory feedback. Do not average away a critical blocker or select only favorable quotes. Scores organize qualitative review; there is **no aggregate pass threshold** and no score changes Gate A or authorizes Phase 1.

Reviewer role/domain: ___  Date: ___  Session/log reference: ___  Scope/questions skipped: ___

**Scale:** `0` = concrete objection or infeasible in reviewed context; `1` = serious unresolved concern; `2` = mixed, conditional, or insufficient evidence; `3` = plausible in reviewed context with stated limits; `4` = supported by a concrete reviewed example in that context. `N/A` = not discussed; do not infer a neutral score. These are reviewer assessments, not product verdicts. **Confidence:** low / medium / high, based on the reviewer's direct knowledge of that dimension. **Thesis impact:** supports / weakens / does not affect / unknown. **Blocker:** none / conditional / critical; classify a critical blocker when the objection defeats the proposed workflow or trust boundary in the reviewed context, even if other scores are high.

For **critical blocker severity**, score `0` = no blocker identified; `1` = minor repair within current design; `2` = material unresolved issue; `3` = blocks use in reviewed context; `4` = appears to defeat a core baseline premise. A high number here is adverse. This is a severity assessment, not a claim that the baseline has changed.

Claim types for each summary: **CURRENT FACT** (link to repository decision), **REVIEWER OPINION**, **OBSERVED EXPERIENCE** (reviewer's account, scope and limits), **HYPOTHESIS**, **UNKNOWN**, or **PUBLIC SOURCE CLAIM** (URL, not yet independently reviewed). Quote only what was actually said; mark paraphrases. Record dissent and counterexamples in the [decision log](expert-review-decision-log.md). Multiple reviewers may disagree; retain individual rows and contexts. No interview score, even `4`, establishes a qualifying incident, portability, product readiness, expert-validation completion, or market validation.
